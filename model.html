The model

The AI behind the scan.

EpivaScan runs on an image-classification model that looks at a photo of skin and scores it against a set of conditions. This page is where its details will live.

Planned pipeline

What happens to a photo.

  1. 01

    Photo

    An image of the skin area, taken in the app or uploaded.

  2. 02

    Prepare

    The image is resized and normalised into the format the model expects.

  3. 03

    Classify

    The model scores the image against each condition it was trained on.

  4. 04

    Report

    Scores are shown as percentages, highest first.
