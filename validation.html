Shaped in the clinic, with dermatologists.

EpivaScan is being developed alongside skin doctors in Bangkok. Each consultation runs the same way.

  1. Walk through the app

    The dermatologist sees the whole flow, from adding a photo to reading the matches.

  2. Read the matches together

    We compare the app’s results on skin photos with how they read the same image.

  3. Take it back to the build

    Their notes decide which conditions to cover, how results are worded, and when to send someone to a clinician.
