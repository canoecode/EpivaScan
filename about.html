Our story

For years, I lived with an inflammatory eye disease called vernal keratoconjunctivitis. For more than a year, I visited doctors without getting an answer about what was causing my symptoms. I had to manage the pain and inflammation without knowing what condition I was dealing with or how it could be treated.

Once I finally received a diagnosis, I could begin the right treatment. That experience made me think about the millions of people living with undiagnosed skin conditions, many of which can be treated once properly identified. Yet billions of people live far from a dermatologist and have limited access to specialist care. I created EpivaScan to help close that gap, allowing people to take a photo of their skin, receive an initial AI assessment, and get guidance on when to seek medical attention.

After developing EpivaScan and TeleSync, I founded Pulma to make early lung cancer screening possible through a simple breath test. While each project addresses a different health problem, they all stem from the same motivation I had after my own diagnosis. I want people to have access to the information they need to recognise a health problem early, regardless of where they live.

FAQ

Does EpivaScan diagnose skin disease?

No. It shows which conditions a photo most closely resembles, with a percentage for each. That is a starting point for a conversation with a health professional, who makes the diagnosis.

Can I use it today?

Yes, as a demo. The web demo sends your photo to the EpivaScan model and shows what it finds. EpivaScan is still in development and has not been tested in a clinic, so treat a result as a starting point, not an answer.

Which conditions will it recognise?

Thirteen for now: eczema, contact dermatitis, hives, insect bites, folliculitis, psoriasis, ringworm (tinea), cold sores, shingles, acne, impetigo, pityriasis rosea and drug rashes. Anything else is reported as “Other / unclear”. How well it does on each is on the model’s page.

What happens to my photo?

The web demo sends your photo to the EpivaScan model, which runs on Hugging Face. The photo is held in temporary storage while it is analysed and deleted within about twenty minutes. It is not stored anywhere else or used for training. A full privacy policy is still to come.

Does it work on a computer as well as a phone?

Yes. EpivaScan is designed for phones first and also runs in a web browser on a laptop or desktop.
